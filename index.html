The Role

What is an Introducing Broker?

An introducing broker (IB) is a trusted partner that connects clients to TMGM and earns commissions based on their trading activity. IBs use their network and expertise to earn volume-based commissions on every trade their referred clients make through the IB program.

The program suits financial educators, community leaders and content creators who have an engaged network and a basic understanding of trading.
